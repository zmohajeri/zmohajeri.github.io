Zahra Mohajeri writes fiction, essays and reviews in two languages.

Her English writing, gathered here as **Golden Glyph**, moves between Iran's places, architecture, crafts and food, film and books, media and ideas. In Persian, as **زرنوشت**, she writes stories, notes and criticism.

Her first short story collection, *Dar be samt-e birun baz mishavad* (The Door Opens Outward), was published by Goya in the summer of 2025. Its stories follow women facing loneliness, mistrust, and the pressures of family and society, and searching for a self of their own.
