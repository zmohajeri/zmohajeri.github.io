<p class="sp-title">INVISIBLE</p>
<p class="sp-by">written by<br>Zahra Mohajeri</p>
<p class="sp-trans sp-in">FADE IN:</p>
<p class="sp-scene">EXT. UNIVERSITY YARD - DAY</p>
<p class="sp-action">A quiet university yard. A GIRL (early 20s) sits alone on a bench, absorbed in a book.</p>
<p class="sp-action">A BOY (early 20s), a student, walks over and sits down next to her. Without looking up, she gathers her things and moves them to her other side.</p>
<p class="sp-action">The boy glances at her book. He's cheerful, excited.</p>
<p class="sp-char">BOY</p>
<p class="sp-dial">It's a beautiful day, isn't it?</p>
<p class="sp-char">GIRL</p>
<p class="sp-paren">(after a pause)</p>
<p class="sp-dial">Mm-hmm.</p>
<p class="sp-action">She keeps reading. He slides closer.</p>
<p class="sp-char">BOY</p>
<p class="sp-dial">Have you ever thought there might be invisible people around us?</p>
<p class="sp-char">GIRL</p>
<p class="sp-paren">(looking up)</p>
<p class="sp-dial">Excuse me?</p>
<p class="sp-char">BOY</p>
<p class="sp-dial">Like right now. We're talking to each other, but there could be invisible people all around us.</p>
<p class="sp-action">The girl looks across the yard. It's empty.</p>
<p class="sp-char">BOY (CONT'D)</p>
<p class="sp-dial">It must be so hard for them, that nobody sees them. Don't you think?</p>
<p class="sp-char">GIRL</p>
<p class="sp-paren">(after a pause)</p>
<p class="sp-dial">Maybe. Yes, I suppose so.</p>
<p class="sp-char">BOY</p>
<p class="sp-dial">And you know what's even worse?</p>
<p class="sp-action">She closes her book.</p>
<p class="sp-char">GIRL</p>
<p class="sp-dial">What?</p>
<p class="sp-char">BOY</p>
<p class="sp-dial">When they realize nobody can see them. At first they think they're normal. Then, little by little, they understand they're invisible.</p>
<p class="sp-action">The girl shakes her head but says nothing. She opens her book again.</p>
<p class="sp-char">BOY (CONT'D)</p>
<p class="sp-dial">Do you know what's worse than being invisible?</p>
<p class="sp-action">She snaps the book shut, irritated.</p>
<p class="sp-char">GIRL</p>
<p class="sp-dial">No. What?</p>
<p class="sp-char">BOY</p>
<p class="sp-dial">When the person you love doesn't see you, and sees the most ordinary guy in the world instead.</p>
<p class="sp-char">GIRL</p>
<p class="sp-dial">What's that supposed to mean?</p>
<p class="sp-action">The boy bursts out laughing.</p>
<p class="sp-char">BOY</p>
<p class="sp-paren">(laughing)</p>
<p class="sp-dial">I'm one of them! One of the invisible ones. Can you believe it?</p>
<p class="sp-action">The girl narrows her eyes at him. He slips a hand into his pocket.</p>
<p class="sp-char">BOY (CONT'D)</p>
<p class="sp-dial">I want to show you something.</p>
<p class="sp-action">He pulls out his phone and holds up a photo. The girl leans in, cautiously.</p>
<p class="sp-action">ON THE SCREEN: a heavyset young man with thinning hair.</p>
<p class="sp-action">The girl freezes. She knows this face.</p>
<p class="sp-char">BOY (CONT'D)</p>
<p class="sp-dial">You see? The girl I love sees this guy. Not me. Whatever I did, she never noticed me. Tell me why. Because I'm invisible?</p>
<p class="sp-action">He lets out an unhinged laugh.</p>
<p class="sp-char">BOY (CONT'D)</p>
<p class="sp-dial">Maybe nobody knows this, but invisible people have only one way to become visible.</p>
<p class="sp-action">He grins and raises his other hand. It is covered in something dark red.</p>
<p class="sp-action">The girl stares at it, terrified. A beat. Then she gasps: it's fresh blood.</p>
<p class="sp-action">Two SECURITY GUARDS rush in. Without a word, they seize the boy and twist his arms behind his back. He is still laughing.</p>
<p class="sp-action">An ambulance siren wails, growing louder.</p>
<p class="sp-action">The guards drag him toward the gate. He twists around and shouts back at her.</p>
<p class="sp-char">BOY (CONT'D)</p>
<p class="sp-dial">Now you can see me, right?</p>
<p class="sp-action">He holds up his red hand again.</p>
<p class="sp-char">BOY (CONT'D)</p>
<p class="sp-dial">I'm not invisible anymore!</p>
<p class="sp-action">The guards and the boy disappear through the university gates.</p>
<p class="sp-action">The girl looks down at her book. A single drop of fresh blood has fallen on the white page.</p>
<p class="sp-trans">FADE OUT.</p>
