**A Short Story**

Anna wiped around her lips and stood up, pressing the flush button.

"What happened? Are you better now? " Ilya said loudly.

Anna pushed open the door and walked past Ilya. She lay down on the bed.

"What did you eat that made you feel bad?"  Ilya said as he sat next to her on the bed.

Anna stared at the bright light bulb over her head. She had a strong desire to buy a pink chandelier for the room. One of those gleaming multi-branched chandeliers she passed daily in the lighting shop. "I wish we could switch out this light. It is way too dim," Under her breath, she murmured. Ilya raised his head and lifted his eyes to the ceiling, "We shall get it. All of the money from winning this award is yours. Buy whatever appeals to you."

Ilya got up from bed: "I will prepare a banana milkshake for you. You must strengthen yourself more quickly. Tomorrow is the last training day, hero!" He walked out of the room.

Anna stood up and followed Ilya. Ilya peeled bananas and tossed them into the mixer.

"What if I do not compete this time? If I don't want to compete for a while." She said, sitting at the counter next to Ilya.

Ilya poured the milk into the mixer bowl and drizzled some honey: "It's a simple case of food poisoning, my dear. You’ll get better soon." He switched the device on.

"If you win this battle, you will be the first female to defeat Jonathan!" He remarked. "When you win, I will leap into the cage and climb atop him, saying, “How are you, little mouse?” After that, I will raise you to my shoulders and proclaim “This heroine who defeats all men in the cage, is my girl!” he said with a smile and switched off the mixer.

"You know what, Anna?" Ilya continued, "I had imagined today ever since I met you. I could see your valour." He poured the banana milkshake into a large glass and slid it across the counter toward Anna.

Anna put the glass to her lips, but she didn't like the smell. "I’m unable to compete, Ilya!" She said quietly. "I'd like to take some time off."

Ilya frowned, "What do you mean you don't want to compete?” He said.

Anna stared at the black coffee stain on the table. "That is what I meant. Everything has so far been to your delight. Now I assert I am unable to."

Ilya sat on a chair beside Anna. He grabbed Anna's wrist and brought it up to his lips. "For this battle, I prepared these fists. What exactly are you up to, my hero?!"

Anna looked at him. "Ilya, am I important to you? Is my desire meaningful to you? Is my comfort a priority for you?”

She yanked her hand away from Ilya's. Ilya pulled back the chair and stood up swiftly, exhaling: "Do you believe this is just a personal decision for you, Anna? We have a goal! Have you forgotten?!

The big coffee stain caught Anna's attention as she turned her face away from Ilya. “I've just followed what you've stated so far. Now I want a sense of peace and tranquillity in my life. I'd rather crawl into a corner and stay there for a while. Like every other woman", Anna said.

"Do you want a quiet life?" Ilya said, tucking his fingers into his hair. " Did you believe you were a common woman?”

Anna poured the milkshake into the sink, "I want to be now. Is there a problem?", She said.

Ilya grinned: "When did you become so commonplace that you desired now? Do you think I love you for your beauty? No honey! All you have is the ability to fight and earn a title. Do not overlook your one and only advantage over other women!" He grabbed his sweater, exited the flat, and shut the door intensely.

Anna leaned against the counter. She didn't seem to be able to move. She wanted to cry but her tears remained imprisoned in an unknown place, refusing to flow. She grabbed a little pink kit from her pocket and stared at the two purple stripes that were becoming brighter by the minute.
