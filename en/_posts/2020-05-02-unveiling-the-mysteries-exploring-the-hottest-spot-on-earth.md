In each region of four-season Iran, there is a kind of natural wonder that is different from other regions. Tourists can reach the most deserted areas from the verdant lands in a short distance. In the meantime, it is worth to know the hottest spot on the earth better, which its temperature reaches more than 70 °C on some days of the year.

If you travel to the Kalut Shahdad Desert in Kerman, you will encounter top-flat reliefs while on the other side, hilly reliefs could be seen. Kalut is a natural and geographical phenomenon in the Lut Desert of Iran, which has been created by water, wind, and soil erosion for 20,000 years.

The Kalut Shahdad is located in an area of more than 11,000 (sq. km) in the middle of the Lut Desert. According to geologists, this region is one of the geothermal poles of the earth, which has made it very attractive to domestic and foreign tourists.

Tourists who travel to this area think that the dunes are man-made, but the fact is that natural phenomena have created this tourist attraction, and according to experts the 120-day winds blowing from Sistan were the cause of its formation. Other experts believe that the Shoor River also contributed to the creation of these phenomena. This river flows in the middle of the Lut desert and its headwater is the Birjand mountains; then it reaches the salt dome and southeast. According to tourists and dessert lovers, the dunes in Kalut Shahdad are unrivaled in the world, both in number and shape, and size. From a far view, it sounds like a city made up of numerous towers and houses.

Lut desert-Kalut Shahdad is the hottest place in Kerman

Kalut Shahdad is located around Shahdad city in Kerman province. This region, which is one of the lowest points on the Iranian plateau, is only 56 meters above sea level. The temperature registered by NASA for the Lut desert was 70.7 °C which is a kind of record. This record is one of the most important reasons that attract tourists to Kalut Shahdad.

Although no living species are found in this area, a historical study shows that life has existed in this area for many years. According to archaeologists, the history of its urbanization dates back to more than 6,000 years ago.

In the south of Kalut Shahdad, objects have been founded that indicate civilization in this region. Since these objects have been discovered in the mud and salt, archaeologists believe that human gatherings may have been destroyed by an accident such as devastating floods in the area.

There is not much vegetation in this area too, And the reason is the very strong winds that can uproot any plant and destroy it.

Due to its vastness and type of geography, Kalut Shahdad is divided into three areas: green, orange, and red for tourists. In the green area, which is about 2 km far from the Shahdad-Nehbandan road, tourists are allowed to enter and cross the desert. But in the orange and red areas, it is not possible to travel without a guide and device, and they may get lost or stuck in the sand.

To reach Kalut Shahdad, you must first go to Kerman and then Nehbandan city and from there enter the Kerman-Bam road. A little later, the signs that guide you to the Kalut Shahdad will be seen.

Visiting Kalut Shahdad is recommended both for those who are interested in desert and desert climbers, and for those who like desert-specific recreations such as safari, off-road, and camel riding. One of the most beautiful landscapes of this region, in addition to the sand dunes, is the desert sky at night and the appearance of the stars that attract tourists’ attention.
