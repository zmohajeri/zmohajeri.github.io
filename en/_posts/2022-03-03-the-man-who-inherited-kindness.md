In our unkind world, some people are trying to make it beautiful. “Johnny Barnes” was one of those who spread love and kindness to everyone making the world a better place. 

Matt Morris’s short documentary, “*Mr. Happy Man”,* reflects on the life of Johnny Barnes, the heartbeat of Hamilton, Bermuda, famed for sharing love among its residents. The documentary opens with a happy soundtrack and shots of crowded streets. A grinning old man, Johnny Barnes, enters the movie with his hands extended, saying he loves rushing commuters and wishing them a very good day. At first, audiences are not aware of who he is or why he behaves that way. Is he crazy? Does he ask for money? Johnny starts to introduce himself in a meaningful way: “ I am just a little small instrument in the hand of God to be used any way he feels fit…”. In this introduction, the director reveals that Johnny has a precious mission in life, and he only wants an open heart in return; neither money nor praise.

*“Mr. Happy Man”* is a short documentary that focuses on its fascinating protagonist. The way Matt Morris chose to create this movie is by interviewing different people about Johnny Barnes as well as Johnny's testimonials about his mission, happiness, and making love. People in the movie claim that they had forgotten how much fun life can be and how many people and things they love, but Johnny reminds them that love and happiness still exist. As one of the residents says: “He reminds me that I have a choice for my day to smile and be happy or carry the anger with me.” Moreover, the documentary demonstrates Johnny's importance to the residents. His presence is not just a daily sight, but also a sign of good fortune. For example, one resident recalls her memorable day when she wanted to deliver her first child. “We were driving to the hospital. I did my normal way to Johnny and he didn't see me. I got nervous, so I made my husband drive around the roundabout till Johnny could see me. To me that felt like it was a good omen”, she says. The people seem to perceive Johnny as a representative of God who bestows mercy.

![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/12e485_9f12f4d29e674e3d8ae4103a90a77ced~mv2.jpg)

Despite the simplicity of the cinematography, the director conveys a powerful message through shots of green trees and sunlit skies overhead, as well as Johnny's narration regarding "life is sweet and beautiful", which adds vibrant color and a well-structured narrative to the movie. 

An installation of a statue of Hamilton's famous resident concludes the documentary. This statue serves as a reminder that there is something that encourages people to be happy and loved.

This inspiring short documentary was not just about Johnny, a kind man who likes to spread love. There is a deeper insight in the movie, and it is to show that a single act of kindness can have enormous effects. Love is contagious, so why do we withhold it from one another?
