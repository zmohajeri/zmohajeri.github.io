Beautiful Iran with several thousand-year histories is the land of many endless wonders! After years, explorers still discover unknowns across this country and amaze the world.

One of these wonderful areas is the underground city. So far, many underground cities have been discovered in Iran and each of them has its own unique features.

Archaeologists believe that the main function of underground cities that were built in the depth of the earth by the people of that time, is to defend against enemy aggression. The reason is that different tribes have invaded the country from time to time in different eras. Therefore, underground cities have made it easier for their residents to defend themselves.

‘’Kordoliya’’ is one of the underground cities that was discovered less than five years ago and was exposed to visitors. This underground city, which is one of the environs of ‘’Karvan’’ district of Tiran city in Isfahan province, has an area of more than 20,000 m² and is one of the most interesting and amazing underground cities in the world.

This underground city is located in the village of Kordoliya. This village has been one of the Armenian villages of Tiran and Karvan cities from the Safavid era until one hundred years ago which has gradually become a Muslim village. In this village, an Armenian cemetery remains, which has also been nationally registered.

![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/12e485_7c4e25a7ed454226a8baee00a0261a7b~mv2.webp)

According to experts, the historical background of Kordoliya dates back to the Parthian era in terms of architecture, arches, and interior space. It seems that this place which is located in the depth of ground probably had military-security usage as well as protection against extreme cold and bad weather. Evidence shows that after a while, during the Safavid period, Armenians who migrated to Isfahan settled in the village of Kordoliya and used this underground city to keep their livestock and protect themselves from the extreme cold weather. In this city, there is a large carved stone that the Armenians seem to have filled with water and baptized in it.

Kordoliya is built in a circle shape at a depth of eight meters under the ground. One of its wonders is the eighty entrances on the ground, rooms, and nested corridors. The city has two floors and about eighty wells with a depth of twelve to eighteen meters have been discovered that still have water. It sounds that these large rooms and halls have been used many times in the post-Parthian period and have had various uses.

Since it is an underground city, there is a dark atmosphere and some remaining items on the walls, show there was a place for tallow lamps at regular intervals. Required Oxygen was supplied through inlet wells. More interestingly, the entrances to the city, which are only one meter apart, are built towards sunrise and are completely hidden from sight view. With this unique design, the city was protected from enemy infiltration and it was difficult to find its main routes.

The 2,000-year-old city, ‘’Kordolia’’ had a capacity of 30,000 warriors, which due to its amazing architecture, is compared to the Catacombs or the underground burial places in Rome, Italy. This city was discovered in 2016 and less than 2,000 meters of it are prepared to visit. To reach this underground city, you need to travel to Isfahan province and from there go to Tiran and Karvan cities and then to Kordolia village.
