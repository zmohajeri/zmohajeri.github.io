Researchers have reported the historical background of urban life in Ardabil eight thousand years ago. This background makes this city and the surrounding areas one of the oldest residential places in Iran. A city that has always been prosperous due to the favorable climate and is located on the Silk Road. Before and after Islam, this city was the center of Azerbaijan and during the Abbasid period, it was the center of the Babak Khorramdin uprising, which, unfortunately, during the Mongol invasion in the seventh century, many ancient civilization relics were destroyed in Ardabil. This beautiful region of Iran, which reaches the Caspian Sea on one side and Sabalan mountain on the other, is full of surprises. In the following, we will mention some of the tourist areas of this beautiful and important city.

![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/12e485_eb3f7a38ec4c436b8e9fb6e3b2cb0426~mv2.webp)

**Friday Mosque**

According to historical reports, the Ardabil Grand Mosque was built exactly on the site of a pre-Islamic building. It is thought that there used to be a fire temple in this place where people worshiped and after Islam in Iran and the conversion of people to Islam, this place has become a mosque. This mosque is located in the east of the city and on a small hill. The current building was built in the fifth century AH and probably on a building of the Sassanid period. Another historical significance of this holy place dates back to the period of the Mongol invasion, in which the destructive attack of this place was not spared from the attack of the invaders and it was seriously damaged. In that attack, the dome of the mosque was destroyed and rebuilt after a while.

**Shater Tower**

“Shater Tower” is One of the oldest Iran historical places in Ardabil. This building is located at a distance of about six kilometers from Ardabil and is located next to a village called “Soume’e”. Several ancient hills around it are of interest to many archaeologists. Scholars do not date these historical hills to pre-Islamic times. One of its unique features is that it has a cylindrical shape inside and a rectangular shape on the outside. The tower building itself is estimated to be around the eighth century AH, as it has many similarities in design and materials to the tomb of Chalabi Oghlou in Soltaniyeh.

**Tomb of Amin Al-din Jabraeil**

The mausoleum and tomb of “Sheikh Amin al-Din Jabraeil “, the father of Sheikh Safi al-Din Ardabili, is one of the examples of architectural masterpieces in the Safavid period. The beauty along with the simplicity of appearance and the balance of the building components on the one hand and the extraordinary decorations of its interior surfaces on the other hand has attracted many art lovers' attention. This tomb is unequally divided into two parts. A central rectangular mansion and a quadrangular building in front of it. The sides of this building, which is located on a stone foundation, have sharp arches.

**Sabalan Mountain**

Sabalan Mountain is the third highest mountain in our country, Iran. A mount with a height of 4811 meters, which is located in the west of Ardabil province. Sabalan is a semi-active volcanic mountain, so numerous hot springs flow around it. The use of hot springs and benefiting from the medicinal properties of these mineral waters can be one of the useful and memorable pleasures of any trip. Some consider Sabalan as the place of resurrection of the prophet Zoroaster and consider it sacred and swear by it. Nietzsche, the famous philosopher, in his book “Thus Spoke Zarathustra” refers to the descent of Zarathustra from Mount Sabalan.

**Tomb of Sheikh Safi al-Din Ardabili**

The tomb of Sheikh Safi, which is one of the unique and important buildings of the Islamic architecture period, includes several complexes that were first unified by Shah Tahmasb and became present during the reign of Shah Abbas Safavid by adding several buildings and restorative reforms. The special importance of this building along with the features of architectural art is its historical importance. The predecessors of the Safavid dynasty several kings, including Shah Ismail I, the founder of the Safavid dynasty, are buried in this tomb. The chandelier, porcelain house, library, and Janat Sara mosque are some of the spectacular parts of this complex.

Ardabil has many places of interest and buildings for tourism; Which mentioning and introducing each of them requires more time. Among the places of interest are the historical bridges of Ardabil (such as Haft Cheshmeh Bridge, Yaghoubiyeh Bridge, Naderi Bridge, and Red Bridge) as well as its historical baths (such as Haj Sheikh Bath, Zahir al-Islam Bath, Mirza Habib Bath, and Zeynal Bath) as well.

Ardabil museums (such as Natural History Museum, Anthropology Museum, Handicrafts Museum, Honors Museum, and Ardabil Stone Museum) as well as historical caravanserais (such as Abbasi Naqdi Caravanserai, Sarbaz Vatan Caravanserai, and Shour Gol Caravanserai) as well as Ardabil Historical Houses (Aghazadeh House, Arshadi, Khadem Bashi and Shariat House) It is very convenient to visit one or more of these houses, museums, bridges, and baths on a recreational trip to this historical city by the available time and the route.
