BBC Persian has had a great deal of influence on Iranian culture, politics, and society since 1941. At first, BBC Persian was just a radio station. As part of a strategic political alliance between the UK and the Persian-speaking world, the Persian Service radio was established during the final years of the Second World War. For the past 66 years, many Iranians have viewed the Persian Service with suspicion, but at the same time have viewed it as one of their most trusted sources of news and information.

In the Iranian revolution of 1979, local and international media played a crucial role in its formation and dynamics. In those days, BBC Persian was the only source of information because Radio Iran was on strike. People can keep up with current events thanks to BBC Persian. Iranian broadcasting has been state-controlled since the revolution and reflects the official ideology.

Because censorship occurs so routinely in domestic news sources and channels, the vast majority of people know the truth is not being reported. As a result, they seek out alternative sources of information. Among foreign channels, this is by far the greatest opportunity to reach an audience. In the past, Iranians were not aware of the thousands of voices against the government. This was due to the omission of their ideas in the domestic media by the regime's propaganda. With BBC Persian, listeners can hear expert opinions on politics, economics, and social issues.

Iran has no knowledge of certain contemporary and sometimes controversial social issues such as grief, aging, fertility, body image, religion, and fashion that BBC covers. Also, Iran's government forbids the discussion of some content due to political and religious reasons. BBC’s Documentaries cover taboo topics to inform people

In the domestic press, for example, homosexuality isn't discussed or written about. Because of this, individuals with this orientation are unable to access relevant information. The BBC, a media outlet that does not have any limitations in describing facts, can assist people in finding information about this topic. Today they dare to reveal their orientation as they know it is acceptable all over the world.  

In addition, BBC Persian TV has broadcast programs on the Holocaust and films about Israeli life. Many documentaries are presented by women on equal terms with their male counterparts. These subjects would never be covered by the Iranian media.

The Iranian people are now more aware of their rights and have a lot of useful information because of foreign Persian channels like BBC Persian. The result occasionally is civil disobedience and protest, which can lead to progressive change.
