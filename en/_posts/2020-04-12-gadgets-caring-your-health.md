Today’s life in a modernized society with a large volume of business and speedy work caused less attention to human health. However, in this modern world, some new technologies are developed those alert people that having a healthy body is the most important thing. Using such technologies, our lifeless friends, would improve our lifestyle and draw more attention to ourselves.

**Air pollution Alarm**

Air pollution is a problem in many large and industrial cities around the world. Although this type of pollution is hazardous for all people, it makes more serious problems for specific groups of people: elder people, special patients with asthma and allergy, and pregnant women are exposed to larger amounts of damage stemming from air pollution. This gadget of air pollution measurement could assist these people to be aware of the contaminant volume at various times per day. This gadget receives analyses and sends air quality information to your smartphone. When the user is exposed to dangerous air pollution, this gadget will ask him/her to leave that place immediately by sending an alert. Also, this gadget measures the temperature and humidity of the weather around the user and informs him/her.

**Smart fork**

Physicians have always alarmed people about their feeding styles and their effects on their digestion. Smart fork, as it could be guessed, considers user’s feeding styles. An acceleration meter is attached to this fork which measures the hand speed during the time of eating food. If the user has a higher speed, the fork will vibrate and alert the user to modify his/her eating method by turning on a red light on it. This smart fork shows all information and reports of a user’s eating styles through a specific application on Android and non-Android smartphones.

**Loyal accompany to quit smoking**

Smoking endangers body health in addition to its high volume of expenditures. Some smokers who have tried to quit smoking could satisfy that it is not easy at all. One of the most interesting wearing gadgets which could be named as a loyal friend who cares about the user is the quit smoking gadget. This technology is attached to the user’s arm which decreases his/her intention of smoking by spreading nicotine. There are some nicotine cartridges in this gadget that the user should define their amount based on his/her consumption pattern. As a result, the user will get up with a proportionate quantity of nicotine every morning which leads to a lower level of intention to smoke.

Helping to correct sitting behind the computer

In many administrative careers, there is a mandate to sit on a chair and work with computers or systems for several hours. Therefore, it is very important to sit on the chairs perfectly. In case of carelessness to this point, there is a probability of hurting to spine and other parts of the body. This gadget of correct sitting alarm could assist its user a lot in this field. This gadget is something like a small pin that is attached to the user’s clothes and controls his/her situation of standing and sitting. It will vibrate and remind to change the situation whenever the user doesn’t sit properly and there is a probability of hurting its body. Moreover, this gadget provides some information and reports of hours of improper situations of the user through its connection with the smartphone.

**Controlling heart health situation**

Tensions in some fields such as job, education, or family areas, could impose pressure on the heart which may lead to some heart disease that raises one’s concerns. One of the applied gadgets in the sphere of human healthcare is the one that is developed to control the health of the heart. This gadget is an instrument to get electrocardiography in a simple way and in any condition. This gadget is portable and light which enables its user to react quickly in case of any heart arithmetic to carry out any required actions. This gadget needs only thirty seconds to give electrocardiography results.
